Formulated from DP's essays · 2023–2024

The Philosophy of Ego Maturity

The human being is a survival-driven creature. Once it realises it is a separate entity, that instinct builds an “I”, and most of what we call meaning, virtue, happiness, philosophy and religion is the “I” defending and extending itself. Freedom does not come from suppressing the ego or from borrowing someone else's wisdom. It comes when the ego is allowed to mature until it has nothing left to defend. Then, in DP's words, “when the ego matures enough, it neutralizes itself.”

The three layers of the mind

The model behind every essay. A single thought has to cross all three layers, and each layer changes it on the way.

Outer conscious mind Ego defences, the senses, and fitting in with society. Pleasure lives here, effortlessly in the present moment, and briefly.
Ego-personality The “I”: uniqueness, opinions, self-esteem and the alpha drive. It always moves on to the next moment.
Psycho-philosophical mind · the self Partly instinct, partly formed in early childhood. It knows it is a single entity responsible for its own existence. Real wisdom is realised here, and this is where existential anxiety comes from.
